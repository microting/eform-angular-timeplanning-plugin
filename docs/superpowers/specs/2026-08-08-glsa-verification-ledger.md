# GLS-A claim verification ledger

**Date:** 2026-08-08
**Spec reference:** W3 of the GLS-A findings verification audit (branch `docs/glsa-findings-verification`).
**Source of claims:** `docs/superpowers/specs/overenskomst-research-findings.md` (this branch).
**Preset/day tagging reference:** `docs/superpowers/specs/sources/CODE-TRUTH.md` (used only to name the preset key and day code each claim touches — code-state statements are not themselves ledger rows).

**Scope.** A row is any assertion in the findings document about (a) what a collective agreement says, (b) what a preset encodes *relative to* the agreement, or (c) a decided interpretation. In scope: the 18 numbered defects in IMPLEMENTATION STATUS, the § 50 praktikant section, the "Normal daglig arbejdstid" decision section, the OK26 verification notes, the GLS-A parts of "Overarbejde × forskudt tid", the § 50 encoding-audit table, and the Ready-to-Implement GLS-A subsections (summary level). Out of scope and deliberately absent: KA/Krifa and the non-GLS-A sectors, pure code-state statements (covered by CODE-TRUTH.md), and process/planning notes.

**Verdict vocabulary** (for W4/W5):

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `CONFIRMED` | Primary agreement text supports the claim as written. |
| `REFUTED` | Primary text contradicts the claim. |
| `PARTIAL` | Substantively right but overstated, mis-scoped, or wrong in a detail. |
| `UNVERIFIABLE` | No accessible primary source settles it (member-only, unpublished, or genuinely silent text). |
| `TBD` | Not yet adjudicated. |
